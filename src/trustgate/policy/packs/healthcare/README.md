# Healthcare

This pack prioritises injection and critical findings on critical production
assets, known exploitation, and reachable high findings. It does not infer what
data a finding can access; provide asset and runtime context explicitly.

The included test exercises the critical healthcare asset rule. Tailor it to the
system's clinical safety, privacy, availability, and data-handling obligations.

Automated evidence does not guarantee compliance with health, privacy, or
medical-device requirements. Use of this policy does not change that limitation.
Qualified legal, safety, privacy, and security review remain necessary.
